08 · MY STORY

More than just a developer.

Five chapters. Some wins, one big setback, and a lot of starting over. Grab a coffee.

01the beginning

A curious mind

I completed my schooling at Santhome Higher Secondary School, and I'm currently pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at Hindustan Institute of Technology and Science.

If there's one thing that has always defined me, it's my desire to seek knowledge from everything around me. I love discovering how things work, understanding new concepts, and exploring beyond what I already know. But my journey into tech wasn't as straightforward as you might imagine.

02school days

A school life full of achievements

I was one of those students who genuinely enjoyed studying. Securing the first rank was something I consistently worked towards, and I rarely found myself outside the top spot. People often called me a nerd — and honestly, I didn't mind. I was proud of the effort I put in.

And I didn't stop at textbooks. I went on to clear the Hindi exams all the way up to the Pandit level — though, full honesty, if you asked me to speak a single Hindi word right now, I'd just smile and change the topic. 😅 I also finished my English typewriting course at Senior Grade with distinction, so my fingers were flying across keyboards long before I ever owned a computer.

Those years taught me discipline, consistency, patience, and to put real effort into everything I do.

03the funny part

The funny beginning of my tech journey

I didn't grow up with smartphones or computers. I didn't even have my own touchscreen phone during school. And despite being a consistent topper, I walked into my Computer Science degree with almost no practical knowledge of computers.

fun fact ☝️

My first phone was a JioPhone — a gift from my dad for securing first rank in 8th grade. Yes, that's where my mobile journey began!

It took until 2020 for me to finally hold my first real smartphone — a Vivo Y20. For most people it was just a phone. For me, it was a whole new world of apps, the internet, and questions I had never thought to ask before.

College changed everything. Programming, software development, tools, APIs, building projects — every new concept opened another door. I may not have started as a techie, but I'm genuinely enjoying the process of becoming one.

04the hard one

The setback that didn't define me

One of my biggest dreams was to study at an IIT. I appeared for JEE and secured a good percentile — but it wasn't enough for the IIT programme I had dreamed of. I was going through a difficult phase, and it affected my Class 12 performance too.

For someone who had always been academically strong, it was heartbreaking. I felt I had disappointed my teachers, my parents, and most of all, myself.

“One difficult phase cannot decide the rest of my life.”

I took time to heal, gathered myself, and started rebuilding. Today I'm preparing for GATE 2028, hoping to pursue my postgraduate studies at an IIT — this time with more maturity, experience and determination. It isn't a failure; it's the chapter that made me stronger.

05right now

Where I am today

A CSE student with a 9.5+ CGPA, exploring software development, sharpening my programming skills, experimenting with new technologies, and working on projects that turn my ideas into reality.

I don't want to become someone who simply knows how to code. I want to understand technology, solve meaningful problems, build useful products — and never stop learning.

a little something I believe in

You don't have to be the best when you start. You just have to be willing to become better every day.

I'm Praga. Not a finished product. Not a perfect person.
Just a curious mind, a passionate learner, and a work in progress.

The journey continues.
