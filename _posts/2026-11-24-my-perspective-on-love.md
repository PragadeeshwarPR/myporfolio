To me, love is one of the purest feelings in the world. I've never been in a relationship or even dated anyone. Some might find it funny when I say I'm a *one-woman man*, but I genuinely believe in loving one person wholeheartedly.

These days, the meaning of love seems to be fading. People often confuse love with attraction and lust. Genuine connections are becoming harder to find, and sometimes it feels like love has lost its true value.

But despite everything, I haven't lost hope. I still believe that somewhere, someday, I'll find someone who understands the beauty of genuine love.

And mark my words: the girl who enters my life and receives my love will be one of the luckiest people in the world. Not because I'm perfect, but because when I love someone, I'll give her my honesty, loyalty, respect, and everything I have. She won't just be a part of my life; **she will be my world, my happiness, my peace, and everything to me.**

I may be waiting longer than others, but I'd rather wait for something real than settle for something temporary.

Because to me, love is not about how many people you have been with. It's about how deeply you can love one person. ❤️
