Hey, I'm Praga. 👋

If you've read my story, you already know I didn't grow up with computers. My first phone was a JioPhone. I got my first real smartphone, a Vivo Y20, only in 2020. And I walked into a Computer Science degree knowing almost nothing about computers.

So why would someone like that start a blog?

## Because learning is better when you write it down

Every week I learn something new: a programming concept, a tool, an idea from a film, or a lesson from a bad day. Most of it disappears if I don't write it down. This blog is my notebook, except it's public.

> You don't have to be the best when you start. You just have to be willing to become better every day.

That line is the whole point of this space. I'm not writing as an expert. I'm writing as someone who is figuring things out, one step at a time.

## What you'll find here

- **Things I build**: what worked, what broke, and what I learned fixing it.
- **Things I learn**: concepts from my CSE journey and my GATE 2028 preparation, explained the way I wish someone had explained them to me.
- **Things beyond code**: cinema, fitness, and the small lessons that come from just living life.

## A promise

I'll keep it honest. No pretending to know everything. If I got something wrong, I'll say so. If something took me three tries to understand, I'll tell you that too.

If any of this helps even one person who also started late, it's worth it.

Still learning. Still growing. Still becoming.

**— Praga**
